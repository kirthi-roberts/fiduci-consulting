Canada–Asia Business Advisory

Connecting proven Asian manufacturers and technology companies with Canadian buyers, and Canadian solutions with Asian markets, where supply is short or demand is growing fast.

Fiduci’s network across Canadian engineering and technology gives us direct access to the senior technical leaders who specify and buy critical equipment. We help trusted suppliers enter Canada, and help Canadian innovators grow in Asia-Pacific markets.

What We Offer

Two directions. One trusted bridge.

01

Market entry for Asian manufacturers

Reaching Canadian buyers, understanding how Canadian purchasing works, and pricing against local lead times.

02

Commercial representation

Acting as a commission-based agent that introduces companies to developers, distributors, utilities and industrial buyers.

03

Technical credibility

An engineer’s review of product fit, specifications and Canadian standards, speaking to both the supplier’s and the buyer’s engineers.

04

Certification and approvals

Guiding companies through the Canadian electrical, safety and efficiency certifications their products need.

05

Canadian technology into Asia

Matching Canadian products (for example in energy, water, industrial software and climate compliance) with Asian companies facing the same problems.

06

Partnership development

Finding, qualifying and introducing partners between Canada and Asia, drawing on Canada’s trade agreements and strategic partnerships across the Asia-Pacific, including CPTPP and the new Canada–Viet Nam Strategic Partnership.
